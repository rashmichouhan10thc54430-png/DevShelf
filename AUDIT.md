GDGOC AITR Website Audit

1. Overview

- Website: GDGOC AITR
- Website URL: https://gdgocaitr.vercel.app/
- Audit Type: UI/UX, usability, navigation, responsiveness, and content review
- Purpose: To evaluate the website from a visitor's perspective and identify opportunities to improve the user experience.

2. Audit Objectives

The audit focuses on the following areas:

- Navigation and ease of use
- Visual design and readability
- Mobile responsiveness
- Events and club information
- Functionality of buttons and links

3. Evaluation Checklist

Area| What to Evaluate
Navigation| Are menu items easy to find and use?
UI/UX| Is the layout clear and consistent?
Responsiveness| Does the website display correctly on mobile devices?
Content| Is club and event information easy to understand?
Links and Buttons| Do interactive elements lead to the correct destination?

4. Potential Improvements

The following are improvement opportunities to consider after testing the website:

- Make important actions, such as joining the community or exploring events, easy to discover.
- Maintain consistent spacing, typography, and button styles.
- Ensure that content remains readable on smaller screens.
- Keep event details and community information organized.
- Verify that navigation links and interactive elements work as expected.

5. Testing Status

This document provides an initial audit checklist. Individual findings should be recorded only after testing the corresponding website features. No specific functional issue is claimed without verification.

6. Conclusion

A clear, accessible, and responsive website can help students discover GDGOC AITR activities and participate in its community. Testing the website across different devices and checking its interactive elements can help identify practical improvements.
